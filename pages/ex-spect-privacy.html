1. Overview

Ex-Spect ("the Application") is an enterprise business-to-business (B2B) hazardous area (EEHA) inspection, verification dossier, and asset management platform operated by SALCAN Process Technology Pty Ltd (ABN {{ site.salcan.header_ABN }}) ("SALCAN", "we", "us", or "our"), accessible via ex-spect.salcanpt.com and the Ex-Spect Android mobile application. We are committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. Information We Collect

Ex-Spect does not offer open public consumer sign-ups; user accounts are provisioned exclusively by authorised company or platform administrators. To deliver core inspection and engineering compliance functionality, we collect only the following information:

  • Account & Identity Details: Full name, work email address, encrypted authentication credentials, assigned organisation roles (e.g., Technician, Supervisor, Manager, Administrator), and optional profile avatar photo.
  • Inspection & Compliance Records: Hazardous area equipment tag registers, AS/NZS 60079 inspection checklist responses, engineering remarks, remedial action notes, approval timestamps, and user audit logs linking compliance actions to authenticated personnel.
  • Photos & Technical Documents: Photographs of plant equipment, Ex compliance plates, defects, and conformity certificates captured via your device camera or selected from device storage for attachment to inspection records.
  • Location Data (GPS): Device GPS coordinates when explicitly recording or viewing plant equipment tag locations on facility maps. Ex-Spect does not track your location in the background when the app is closed.

3. Mobile Device Permissions (Android)

  • Camera & Media Storage: Used solely when photographing equipment, compliance plates, or defects during an inspection, or when uploading a profile avatar or technical document.
  • Location (Approximate & Precise): Used solely while the app is in active use to plot or capture physical equipment tag coordinates on site maps.
  • Internet / Network Access: Required to authenticate your user session and synchronise inspection records securely with our cloud database.

4. How We Use & Share Information

We use collected information strictly to:

  • Authenticate authorised personnel and enforce multi-tenant Row-Level Security (RLS) access controls.
  • Generate AS/NZS 60079 hazardous area inspection reports, verification dossiers, and asset registers for facility owners.
  • Maintain engineering quality assurance and statutory audit trails.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties, and Ex-Spect contains zero third-party advertising or marketing analytics SDKs. Data is accessible only to the authorised client organisation(s) to which your account is assigned and is hosted on encrypted cloud database infrastructure (Supabase).

5. Data Security & Retention

All communications between the Application and our servers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and stored at rest using enterprise access controls and strict tenant isolation. Equipment inspection histories and verification dossiers are retained in accordance with client contractual obligations and statutory hazardous area safety standards.

6. Account & Personal Data Deletion Requests

Users of the Ex-Spect web portal or Android application may request the deactivation and permanent deletion of their Ex-Spect user account at any time using either method below:

  1. Contact your organisation's designated Ex-Spect Administrator; or
  2. Email SALCAN directly at {{ site.email }} from your registered work email address with the subject line "Ex-Spect Account Deletion Request".

What is deleted vs. retained: Upon verification, your login credentials, email address, profile avatar, and active platform access will be permanently deleted within 14 business days. Historical engineering sign-off records on completed statutory hazardous area inspections required by facility operators under AS/NZS 60079 regulatory obligations are retained as part of the facility's permanent compliance register.
